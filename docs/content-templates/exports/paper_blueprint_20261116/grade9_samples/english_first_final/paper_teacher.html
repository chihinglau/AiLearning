二、单项选择（共 12 题，24 分）

11.（2 分）

Lanterns ___ hung before the festival.

  • A. must be
  • B. are must
  • C. have
  • D. must

【答案】A

【解析】灯笼被挂起是被动，情态动词后用 be done，所以选 must be。

12.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. More and more people in Shenzhen ride bicycles to work. Bicycles are cheap and produce no pollution. However, cyclists often share narrow roads with cars, which can be dangerous. Last year the city built forty kilometres of special lanes. A survey of two thousand riders found that seventy percent of them felt safer after the lanes were built. What problem do cyclists face?

  • A. paying high fees
  • B. finding fuel
  • C. repairing bikes
  • D. sharing narrow roads with cars

【答案】D

【解析】答案依据短文内容：sharing narrow roads with cars。

13.（2 分）

We ___ like cartoons.

  • A. doesn't
  • B. isn't
  • C. don't
  • D. aren't

【答案】C

【解析】第三人称单数否定用 doesn't。

14.（2 分）

The classroom ___ every day by students.

  • A. cleans
  • B. is cleaning
  • C. cleaned
  • D. is cleaned

【答案】D

【解析】主语是被打扫的对象，every day 用一般现在被动 is cleaned，所以选 D。

15.（2 分）

If everyone ___ less, waste will drop.

  • A. threw
  • B. throwing
  • C. will throw
  • D. throws away

【答案】D

【解析】主句将来时，if 从句用一般现在 throws away，所以选 C。

16.（2 分）

This tower is ___ than that one.

  • A. tall
  • B. more tall
  • C. tallest
  • D. taller

【答案】D

【解析】than 前用比较级，tall 的比较级是 taller，所以选 A。

17.（2 分）

  • A. driven
  • B. driving

【解析】表从业者用名词后缀 -er，driver 正确，所以选 B。

三、词汇与句型填空（共 10 题，20 分）

23.（2 分）

They ___ (有) a new computer.

【答案】have

【解析】括号提示「有」，应填 have。

24.（2 分）

My friends ___ (打扫) the floor on Friday.

【答案】clean

【解析】括号提示「打扫」，应填 clean。

25.（2 分）

My friends usually ___ (步行) to the library.

【答案】walk

【解析】括号提示「步行」，应填 walk。

26.（2 分）

The house ___ stands by the river is old.

【答案】that

【解析】从句缺主语、先行词指物，用 that 引导，所以填 that。

27.（2 分）

A paragraph explains why; it gives a ___.

【答案】reason

【解析】解释为何庆祝即给出原因，所以填 reason。

28.（2 分）

People watch the moon ___ Mid-Autumn Day.

【答案】on

【解析】具体某一天用介词 on，所以填 on。

29.（2 分）

The shop is next ___ the old bridge.

【答案】to

【解析】next to 是固定搭配，表紧邻，所以填 to。

30.（2 分）

We should save water ___ it is limited.

【答案】because

【解析】后句给出原因，用 because 连接，所以填 because。

31.（2 分）

Factories must not ___ the river.

【答案】pollute

【解析】must not 后接动词原形，所以填 pollute。

32.（2 分）

This tower has ___ repaired twice.

【答案】been

【解析】has been done 是现在完成被动，所以填 been。
