  • A. cheap and clean
  • B. fast and new
  • C. quiet and small
  • D. easy to park

【答案】A

【解析】答案依据短文内容：cheap and clean。

  • B. dances
  • C. dancing

【答案】Last year I met a student from England. He greeted me with a hug while I shook hands. We talked about our festivals and food. I learned that differences make the world interesting.

【解析】参考范文：Last year I met a student from England. He greeted me with a hug while I shook hands. We talked about our festivals and food. I learned that differences make the world interesting.
