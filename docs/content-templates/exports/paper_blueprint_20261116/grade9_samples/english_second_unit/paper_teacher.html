二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. More and more people in Shenzhen ride bicycles to work. Bicycles are cheap and produce no pollution. However, cyclists often share narrow roads with cars, which can be dangerous. Last year the city built forty kilometres of special lanes. A survey of six hundred riders found that ninety percent of them felt safer after the lanes were built. Why do more people ride bicycles?

  • A. cheap and clean
  • B. fast and new
  • C. quiet and small
  • D. easy to park

【答案】A

【解析】答案依据短文内容：cheap and clean。

10.（2 分）

The children can ___ dance very well.

  • A. danced
  • B. dances
  • C. dancing
  • D. dance

【答案】D

【解析】情态动词 can 后接动词原形，不随主语变化。

11.（2 分）

The children ___ in Beijing.

  • A. live
  • B. living
  • C. lived
  • D. lives

【答案】A

【解析】一般现在时第三人称单数加 -s。

  • C. I am pressure very much.

【答案】A

【解析】under pressure 是固定搭配。
