  • A. is
  • B. was
  • C. be

14.（2 分）

「他正在跑时摔倒了」英文是（ ）

  • A. He was falling while he ran.
  • B. He fell while he runs.
  • C. He fell while he was running.
  • D. He falls while he runs.

【答案】C

【解析】while 引导的从句用过去进行时，主句用一般过去时。
