1.（1 分）

  • A. guide
  • B. souvenir
  • C. journey
  • D. experience

【听力材料】The journey took three days by train.

【答案】C

【解析】录音句子为 “The journey took three days by train.”，其中含 journey。

二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. More and more people in Shenzhen ride bicycles to work. Bicycles are cheap and produce no pollution. However, cyclists often share narrow roads with cars, which can be dangerous. Last year the city built forty kilometres of special lanes. A survey of eight hundred riders found that sixty percent of them felt safer after the lanes were built. What problem do cyclists face?

  • A. sharing narrow roads with cars
  • B. repairing bikes
  • C. finding fuel
  • D. paying high fees

【答案】A

【解析】答案依据短文内容：sharing narrow roads with cars。

三、词汇填空（共 8 题，16 分）

17.（2 分）

My friends ___ (吃) noodles at twelve.

【答案】eat

【解析】括号提示「吃」，应填 eat。

18.（2 分）

My friends ___ (帮助) the old teacher cross the road.

【答案】help

【解析】括号提示「帮助」，应填 help。

19.（2 分）

___ I was reading, my friend called.

【答案】While

【解析】从句是延续动作 was reading，用 While 引导，所以填 While。

20.（2 分）

【答案】go

【解析】括号提示「去」，应填 go。
