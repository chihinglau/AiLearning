二、单项选择（共 12 题，24 分）

11.（2 分）

Which sentence about the clown is correct?

  • A. The clown cans run fast.
  • B. The clown can runs fast.
  • C. The clown can running fast.
  • D. The clown can run fast.

【答案】D

【解析】can + 动词原形。

五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 5 句话写夏天的天气和你做的事。

【答案】It is hot in summer. The sun is bright. I swim with my father. I eat ice cream. Summer is fun.

【解析】参考范文：It is hot in summer. The sun is bright. I swim with my father. I eat ice cream. Summer is fun.

【评分点】描述天气；写出 2 种活动；用 It is ... 句型
