一、听力（共 8 题，8 分）

1.（1 分）

听录音，选出句子中出现的单词。

  • A. thirteen
  • B. number
  • C. thirty
  • D. phone

【听力材料】My phone number is long.

【答案】B

【解析】录音句子为 “My phone number is long.”，其中含 number。

2.（1 分）

  • A. river
  • B. live
  • C. desert
  • D. sea

【听力材料】sea

【答案】D

【解析】录音朗读的是 sea，其余为同单元干扰词。

【听力材料】Where do you live?

二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

My father ___ the news in the evening.

  • A. watched
  • B. watching
  • C. watches
  • D. watch

【答案】C

【解析】以 ch 结尾的动词加 -es。

10.（2 分）

My father ___ very excited today.

  • A. are
  • B. is
  • C. am
  • D. be

【答案】B

【解析】be 动词随主语的人称和数变化。

11.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a small crab near the tree. He put it back because it was alive. Where did Tom go?

  • A. the mountain
  • B. the zoo
  • C. the beach
  • D. the farm

【答案】C

【解析】答案依据短文内容：the beach。

12.（2 分）

My father ___ like reading.

  • A. doesn't
  • B. isn't
  • C. aren't
  • D. don't

【答案】A

【解析】第三人称单数否定用 doesn't。

16.（2 分）

  • A. How much
  • B. How old
  • C. How many
