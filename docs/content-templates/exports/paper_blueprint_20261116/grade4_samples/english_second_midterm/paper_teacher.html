一、听力（共 10 题，20 分）

1.（2 分）

  • B. music

二、单项选择（共 12 题，24 分）

  • B. are
  • C. is
  • D. am

【答案】C

【解析】be good at 为固定搭配。

12.（2 分）

  • A. was
  • B. be
  • C. is

五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 6 句话写你与同学相处的做法。

【答案】I share my books with my friends. We help each other. I say sorry when I am wrong. I say thank you when others help me. We are good friends.

【解析】参考范文：I share my books with my friends. We help each other. I say sorry when I am wrong. I say thank you when others help me. We are good friends.

【评分点】使用 share / help；写 2 种做法；有礼貌用语
