二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a small crab near the bush. He put it back because it was not his. Where did Tom go?

  • A. the zoo
  • B. the beach
  • C. the farm
  • D. the mountain

【答案】B

【解析】答案依据短文内容：the beach。

10.（2 分）

My mother ___ breakfast at seven.

  • A. has
  • B. having
  • C. had
  • D. have

【答案】A

【解析】第三人称单数 have→has。

11.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a small crab near the bush. He put it back because it was too heavy. Where did Tom go?

  • A. the mountain
  • B. the beach
  • C. the park
  • D. the zoo

【答案】B

【解析】答案依据短文内容：the beach。

12.（2 分）

My father ___ many cakes in the bag.

  • A. was
  • B. be
  • C. is
  • D. are

【答案】D

【解析】many 修饰可数名词复数，用 are。

13.（2 分）

My father ___ to the library yesterday.

  • A. goes
  • B. going
  • C. go
  • D. went

【答案】D

【解析】yesterday 提示一般过去时，动词用过去式且不随主语变化。

16.（2 分）

This is my desk. It is red.

  • A. sister
  • B. sisters

四、阅读理解（共 4 题，20 分）

25.（5 分）

根据情景选择最合适的句子：My mother is a doctor.

  • A. The farmer works on a farm.
  • B. My mother is a doctor.
  • C. A driver drives a bus.
  • D. A teacher works in a school.

【答案】A

【解析】该情景最贴切的表达是 “The farmer works on a farm.”。
