三、词汇与句型填空（共 10 题，20 分）

23.（2 分）

They ___ (教) us English.

【答案】teach

【解析】括号提示「教」，应填 teach。

24.（2 分）

【答案】open

【解析】括号提示「打开」，应填 open。

25.（2 分）

Should and ___ share /ʃ/.

【答案】shoe

【解析】shoe 的 sh 与 should 同发 /ʃ/。

26.（2 分）

___ the door in a drill.

【答案】Open

【解析】演练时缓慢开门，祈使句用 Open。

27.（2 分）

【答案】helmet

28.（2 分）

【答案】shouldn't

五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 6–8 句话介绍一座城市。

【答案】This is a big city. There are many streets and parks. There is a museum near the library. The city is busy but clean. I like living here.

【解析】参考范文：This is a big city. There are many streets and parks. There is a museum near the library. The city is busy but clean. I like living here.
