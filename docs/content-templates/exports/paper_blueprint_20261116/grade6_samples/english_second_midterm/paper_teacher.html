二、单项选择（共 12 题，24 分）

11.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a red leaf near the wall. He put it back because it was not his. When did Tom go to the beach?

  • A. last Thursday
  • B. last Monday
  • C. last Tuesday
  • D. last Wednesday

【答案】B

【解析】答案依据短文内容：last Monday。

五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 6–8 句话介绍一本你喜欢的故事书。

【答案】I like the story about five peas. Five peas live in a pod. One pea falls into the soil and grows. In the end it makes a beautiful flower. The story tells us to keep hope.

【解析】参考范文：I like the story about five peas. Five peas live in a pod. One pea falls into the soil and grows. In the end it makes a beautiful flower. The story tells us to keep hope.

【评分点】写出书名与主角；概述情节；说明喜欢的理由
