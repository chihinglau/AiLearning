一、听力（共 8 题，8 分）

1.（1 分）

听录音，选出最合适的应答。

  • A. It's Monday.
  • B. I like apples.
  • C. She is my sister.
  • D. London.

【听力材料】What is the capital of England?

【答案】D

【解析】问句 “What is the capital of England?” 的合适应答是 “London.”。

二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a old coin near the stone. He put it back because it was alive. Where did Tom go?

  • A. the museum
  • B. the farm
  • C. the park
  • D. the beach

【答案】D

【解析】答案依据短文内容：the beach。

10.（2 分）

My mother ___ than my cousin.

  • A. are taller
  • B. is taller
  • C. tall
  • D. taller

【答案】B

【解析】than 提示比较级，且需保留 be 动词。

  • A. last Thursday

12.（2 分）

【答案】D

  • A. Too much sugar is not healthy.
  • B. Children should sleep nine hours.
  • C. Fruit and vegetables are healthy.
  • D. You should exercise every day.

【解析】该情景最贴切的表达是 “You should exercise every day.”。
