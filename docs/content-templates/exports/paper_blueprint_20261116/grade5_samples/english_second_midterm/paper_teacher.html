二、单项选择（共 12 题，24 分）

11.（2 分）

Jack ___ many apples in the bag.

  • A. is
  • B. are
  • C. be
  • D. was

【答案】B

【解析】many 修饰可数名词复数，用 are。

12.（2 分）

This is my bike. It is new.

  • A. sister is
  • B. sister's
  • C. sisters
  • D. sister

【答案】B

【解析】表示「我姐姐的某物」要用名词所有格 sister's。

13.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. Last Monday, Tom and his father went to the beach. The weather was sunny and warm. They collected shells and collected shells. Tom found a little shell near the bench. He put it back because it was too heavy. What was the weather like?

  • A. rainy and cold
  • B. cloudy and cool
  • C. sunny and warm
  • D. clear and hot

【答案】C

【解析】答案依据短文内容：sunny and warm。

五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 6 句话写一项伟大的发明。

【答案】Paper is a great invention. People write and print on paper. The wheel is another great invention. It helps people travel faster. Inventions make our life easier.

【解析】参考范文：Paper is a great invention. People write and print on paper. The wheel is another great invention. It helps people travel faster. Inventions make our life easier.

【评分点】点明发明；说明用途；说明它如何改变生活
