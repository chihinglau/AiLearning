五、书面表达（共 1 题，18 分）

39.（18 分）

用 6 句话写保护地球的做法。

【答案】The Earth is our home. We should protect it. We can recycle paper and bottles. We should not waste water. Small actions make a big difference.

【解析】参考范文：The Earth is our home. We should protect it. We can recycle paper and bottles. We should not waste water. Small actions make a big difference.

【评分点】使用 should / can；写 2 种以上做法；有环保主题词
