二、单项选择（共 8 题，16 分）

9.（2 分）

Read the passage and answer. More and more people in Shenzhen ride bicycles to work. Bicycles are cheap and produce no pollution. However, cyclists often share narrow roads with cars, which can be dangerous. Last year the city built twenty kilometres of special lanes. A survey of six hundred riders found that sixty percent of them felt safer after the lanes were built. What problem do cyclists face?

  • A. finding fuel
  • B. paying high fees
  • C. sharing narrow roads with cars
  • D. repairing bikes

【答案】C

【解析】答案依据短文内容：sharing narrow roads with cars。
